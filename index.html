THE STORY

He forgot his past.
His pain did not.

Setsuna is nineteen. Clever. Outgoing when he chooses to be. A loner by instinct. He can teleport, and a scarred eye hides a power that can copy supernatural abilities.

But the mystery that follows him is more dangerous than any enemy. People, places, voices, and moments trigger pain he cannot explain. Every ache is a fragment of a life his mind can no longer reach.

Somewhere beyond the ruins, kings rule ancient bloodlines, wolves watch from the dark, and someone is afraid that Setsuna will remember.

SETSUNA

THE MEMORYLESS STRATEGIST

Setsuna is confident, clever, observant, and fiercely independent. He studies people before acting, looks for patterns others miss, and would rather solve a problem himself than ask for help.

He is not evil. His moral line is real—but the story will test how long he can keep it.
